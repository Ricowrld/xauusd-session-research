## Expanded project entry

**XAUUSD cross-session research | Python, statistical inference, historical replay**

- Tested six hypothesis families on 2016-2023 bid/ask archive data using causal session features, daylight-saving conversion, HAC inference, false-discovery-rate correction and block-bootstrap uncertainty.
