# Historical XAU/USD quote availability - unsent enquiry template

Subject: Historical XAU/USD bid/ask data suitability for quantitative research

I am conducting academic-style quantitative research on XAU/USD and need to verify
historical data suitability before purchasing access.

Could you confirm:

1. The exact first timestamp for genuine two-sided XAU/USD bid and ask quotes
   available in your archive.
2. Whether coverage is continuous through 31 December 2015, with an inventory of
   known outages or missing periods rather than an archive-wide start date alone.
3. Whether historical data includes genuine quote observations during
   09:00-15:00 Asia/Tokyo and 08:00-12:00 Europe/London.
4. The historical timezone convention and whether DST is represented using
   exchange/local time or UTC.
5. Whether quotes are original observations or may be interpolated, smoothed,
   forward-filled or reconstructed, and whether those records are identifiable.
6. Whether bid and ask timestamps represent contemporaneous observations.
7. Whether contributor/broker sources changed materially over the history and
   whether those changes are documented, including upstream supplier provenance.
8. Whether you can provide a timestamp-only or genuine-quote availability-mask
   sample before purchase, ideally for one historical year before 2016.
9. Whether you can provide a small sample restricted to 2016-2023 so I can compare
   timing and quote construction with an existing research feed.
10. Single-instrument research pricing, licence terms, and whether publication of
    derived statistics and figures is permitted.

I do not need redistribution rights for raw data. Please do not supply any numerical
market-price observations on or after 1 January 2024.

The study needs 4,000 unique fresh scored dates before 2016, plus 400 eligible initial
training dates and feature history. Instrument-level timestamp evidence is needed to
assess that capacity. Please distinguish spot quotes from CFDs, futures, daily
fixings and synthetic histories.
